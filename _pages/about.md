I am an independent AI researcher from India, currently based in **Tomsk, Russia**, working at the intersection of **agentic AI**, **synthetic data generation**, and **efficient machine learning**. My work spans multi-agent LLM orchestration, RLHF/DPO data pipelines, and deployment-focused engineering such as INT8 quantization and MLOps.

At the **AIRI Institute of Artificial Intelligence (TSU x AIRI)** I developed **Auto-ChemInstruct**, an autonomous four-agent pipeline that generates physically verified chemical datasets without human annotators using RDKit and xTB as physical oracles. The manuscript is in preparation for **NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks 2026**. I am currently a **Research Team Lead** at the **Big Mathematical Workshop (BMM)** at Novosibirsk State University, working on **AlignSSL-SV**, a neural system that detects genomic deletions from sequencing read alignments.

Previously, I designed and deployed an inference pipeline for **high-frequency trading market-state forecasting** at **Wunder Fund RNN**, placing in the **Top 30** of the global challenge ranking. I implemented a custom dynamic INT8 quantization pipeline in PyTorch that reduced memory usage by **75%**, allowing a 10-model ensemble to run on a single CPU core under a 20 MB budget. Earlier, at **Turing AI**, I created original competition-style mathematics and algorithmic problems with reference solutions in C++ and Python to train reasoning models.

I hold **3rd place** at **T-Bank Code Gym 2025** (Tomsk) with team *40 + 40 + 1 = 1001*, **4th place** at **Code Sport Tomsk — T-Education 2026** with team *Vibe Compilers*, and a certificate of successful completion from **AvitoTech ML CUP 2025** on Open Data Science (ods.ai). I am pursuing a **Specialist Degree in Mathematics and Computer Science** at **National Research Tomsk State University** (Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, VMiKM), specializing in Computational Mathematics and Computer Modeling.

I am looking for research or ML engineering roles in **Generative AI**, **agentic systems**, or **scientific computing**.
