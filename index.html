About

I'm Ayush, an independent AI researcher from India, currently based in Tomsk, Russia. My work sits at the intersection of agentic AI, synthetic data, and efficient machine learning.

At the AIRI Institute of Artificial Intelligence (TSU × AIRI), I developed Auto-ChemInstruct, an autonomous four-agent pipeline that generates physically verified chemistry datasets without human annotators. The manuscript is in preparation for NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks 2026.

I'm currently Research Team Lead at the Big Mathematical Workshop, building AlignSSL-SV, a neural system for detecting genomic deletions from sequencing read alignments. Before that, I placed in the top 30 of the Wunder Fund RNN Challenge with a dynamically quantized 10-model ensemble that cut memory use by 75%.

I care about systems that are reproducible, auditable, and useful outside a notebook. I'm looking for research or ML engineering roles in generative AI, agentic systems, or scientific computing.
